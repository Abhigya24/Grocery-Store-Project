Introduction


Technology can be defined as the knowledge which is used creatively and how it is applied to organize tasks which involves people and machines that meet sustainable goals. Since ancient times, technology has been around. Based to this concept, early humans discoveries of fire and stone uses represent technological advancements. These days, everything from huge aircraft to tiny semiconductors found in modern computer chips is seen as a technological advancement.

Positive Impact

Now we are no longer bound by physical limitations, and we have a wide range of online grocery stores to choose from. Major food retailers are trying to attract individual customers by offering competitive offers, discounts, loyalty cards, and convenient delivery options. Convenience and efficiency are also determined by the user experience and interaction with each online grocery store, however some buyers just consider pricing and shipping expenses.

1.Home Delivery


One of the major impacts of thechnology is home delivery service.The customers uses a digital platform in order to receive home delivery service. The retailer receives the order and after that, delivery agents receive this order by digital methods. In order to locate their order and communicate with the shop, customers typically use smartphone apps.


2.Artificial Intelligence


Artificial intelligence-based technology has also affected grocery stores all over the world. In order to save clients time during the checkout process, AI technology is employed to detect the products that they place in their carts. After reviewing the product information, this system bills the consumer according to the pricing of the product.

3.Cheaper Price


It is one of of the benefits of technology asOnline stores are very competitive and want to maintain their customer base. To achieve this, they usually sell their products at lower prices than offline stores.Vouchers, festival discount,coupons, are grnerally very helpful for the customers.


4.Variety


Online stores provide a very wide range of products than the offline markets.It provides variety of options to the consumers. The consumer can make better product choice as it offers multiple types of products at one place.




Uses of Technology in Business

    1. It helps you inorder to reduce your in-store losses and theft.

    2. It helps in automation of manual tasks which used to be done in spreadsheets.

    3. It helps in eleminating the potential inventory and pricing errors.

    4. It gives you control to make changes in prices and increase your competitiveness in the market and also increase your profit through it.



Conclusion on impact of technology


In conclusion, the development of technology has changed the grocery shopping experience and is beneficial for both customers and companies. The grocery sector has advanced significantly, from the efficiency of AI-powered checkout systems to the comfort of home delivery services made possible by digital platforms. More options and affordability have been made available to consumers by the availability of lower costs, a wide range of products, and special offers. Technology is now a tool that organizations can use to cut costs, automate processes, reduce errors, and become more competitive in the market. All things considered, the experience of grocery shopping has changed due to the incorporation of technology, becoming more profitable, efficient, and convenient for both consumer and for business as well.
